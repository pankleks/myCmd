# HEIC decoder

HEIC previews use the unmodified WebAssembly decoder from **libheif-js 1.23.5**, licensed under LGPL-3.0. It runs locally; no image data is uploaded.

- Package and build/source instructions: <https://github.com/catdad-experiments/libheif-js/tree/1.23.5>
- libheif source: <https://github.com/strukturag/libheif>
- Included license texts: `LGPL-3.0.txt` and `GPL-3.0.txt`.
